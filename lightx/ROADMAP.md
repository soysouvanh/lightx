# LightX Roadmap: Pure performance, military-grade security & novice accessibility

Although the current architecture of **LightX** lays solid innovative foundations (_Database-First_, _Shift-Left Generation_, _O(1)_ execution), our roadmap focuses exclusively on six vital pillars:

1. **Novice accessibility**: Offer total learning fluidity for beginner developers in the Rust ecosystem.
2. **Craftsmanship quality**: Maintain a codebase with perfect modularity, 100% testable, and ultra-maintainable.
3. **Military surgical precision**: Guarantee infallible application security, down to the pixel.
4. **Absolute performance**: Push execution speed to its SOTA (State of the Art) climax.
5. **Cloud-native extensibility**: Ensure total resilience and interoperability with critical infrastructures (Microservices, very high asynchronous concurrency _Tokio_, OpenTelemetry Observability).
6. **Batteries-included ecosystem**: Natively deliver the DevOps arsenal allowing instant production deployment (integrated CLI, data schema migrations, OpenAPI generated at zero cost).

Here is the mapping of the remaining strategic workstreams to achieve this vision of technical and educational excellence.

- **Specifications (do not interpret):**
  - **Step 1 (`dao_generator.rs` line 679):** In the generation of `RequestContext`, replace `pub raw_parameters: std::collections::HashMap<String, String>` with `pub raw_body: bytes::Bytes`.
